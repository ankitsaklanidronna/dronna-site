Delivery Policy

This policy explains how Dronna delivers purchased digital course access and how we handle activation delays, failed payments, and access support.

1. Digital Delivery Only

Dronna sells online course access, practice sets, mock tests, progress tracking, and related digital learning features. We do not sell or ship physical products.

2. Delivery Method

After successful payment verification through Razorpay, course access is delivered inside your Dronna account. You can access purchased content by logging in with the email address used for the account or purchase.

3. Activation Timeline

  • Most successful payments are activated instantly or within a few minutes.
  • In rare cases involving delayed payment confirmation, activation may take up to 24 hours.
  • If access is not activated within 24 hours after a successful debit, contact support with your Razorpay Payment ID or transaction reference.

4. Failed, Pending, or Duplicate Payments

If payment is marked failed or pending, course access may not be activated until Razorpay or the payment provider confirms success. If the amount was deducted and not refunded by your bank or payment provider, contact us with proof of payment so we can investigate.

5. No Physical Shipping

  • No courier, postal delivery, or physical shipment is involved.
  • No shipping fee, delivery fee, or cash-on-delivery option applies.
  • All access is provided online through dronna.in and the Dronna web app experience.

6. Support for Delivery Issues

For delivery or activation issues, email support@dronna.in with your registered email address, course name, payment date, amount, and Razorpay Payment ID or order ID. We aim to acknowledge access-related issues within 24 to 48 hours.

7. Related Policies

Refunds and cancellations are handled under our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Account and payment-data handling is described in our Privacy Policy.
