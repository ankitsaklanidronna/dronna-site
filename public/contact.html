What to Include

  • Registered Dronna email address.
  • Course name or feature related to your request.

  • Screenshots or error messages if you are reporting an access or technical issue.
  • A short description of what happened and what support you need.

Support Categories

  • Course access: paid access not activated, login problems, or content access issues.
  • Payments: failed, pending, duplicate, or debited transactions through Razorpay.
  • Refunds and cancellations: requests covered under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
  • Privacy: data access, correction, deletion, or account-related privacy requests.
  • Technical help: page errors, app issues, or account sync problems.

Response Timeline

We aim to acknowledge support requests within 2 business days. Payment, refund, and access requests may require additional verification with Razorpay, banks, or payment providers.
