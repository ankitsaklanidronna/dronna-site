Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Dronna collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use dronna.in, create an account, access course content, or make a payment through Razorpay.

Effective Date: May 14, 2026 | Last Updated: May 14, 2026
We collect only the information needed to run the learning platform, provide paid course access, verify transactions, improve service quality, and respond to support requests.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password credentials handled through authentication systems, and exam preference.
  • Learning activity: selected courses, practice attempts, scores, answers, progress, leaderboard activity, and course-access status.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, timestamps, referral URLs, and basic diagnostic logs.
  • Payment confirmation data: Razorpay order ID, payment ID, payment status, amount, currency, and related transaction references.
  • Support communications: messages, attachments, or details you send to us for account, payment, refund, or technical support.

2. Payment Information and Razorpay

Dronna uses Razorpay to process online payments. Sensitive payment instrument details such as card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN, netbanking passwords, wallet credentials, and OTPs are collected and processed by Razorpay or the relevant payment provider. Dronna does not store, copy, or retain customer payment instrument credentials on its servers.

We receive limited transaction confirmation data from Razorpay so that we can verify payment, activate course access, handle duplicate or failed payment issues, maintain accounting records, and respond to refund requests.

By making a payment, you may also be subject to Razorpay's Privacy Policy and applicable payment-provider terms.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your Dronna account.
  • To provide course access, question banks, practice sets, mock tests, progress tracking, and related learning features.
  • To verify payments, activate paid access, issue invoices or receipts where applicable, and resolve payment disputes.
  • To provide customer support, account assistance, refunds, cancellations, and service notices.
  • To improve content quality, platform reliability, fraud prevention, security, and user experience.
  • To comply with applicable laws, payment aggregator requirements, tax/accounting obligations, and valid legal requests.

4. Legal Basis and Consent

We process personal information to provide the services you request, perform our contract with you, comply with legal obligations, prevent misuse, and improve the platform. Where consent is required, you may withdraw it by contacting us, but this may affect our ability to provide certain services.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal data. Information may be shared only with:

  • Payment processors such as Razorpay for payment processing, verification, refunds, dispute handling, and fraud checks.
  • Infrastructure, authentication, database, hosting, analytics, and support tools used to operate Dronna.
  • Professional advisers or authorities where required for legal, tax, accounting, compliance, or security purposes.
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

6. Cookies, Local Storage, and Analytics

Dronna may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember app settings, maintain visitor statistics, improve performance, and understand usage patterns. Disabling these technologies may affect login, progress tracking, and other platform features.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and learning data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Transaction, refund, support, and compliance records may be retained for longer where required by law, accounting rules, dispute resolution, payment-provider requirements, or legitimate business needs.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards such as HTTPS, access controls, authentication, and secure backend services. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so users should also keep their login credentials confidential and report suspicious activity promptly.

9. Your Rights

  • You may request access to the personal information associated with your account.
  • You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • You may request deletion of your account and personal data, subject to legal, payment, accounting, and operational retention requirements.
  • You may withdraw consent for optional communications where applicable.
  • You may raise privacy, payment-data, or account-security concerns using the contact details below.
For privacy requests, account deletion, or payment-data concerns, email support@dronna.in. Please use your registered email address so we can verify the request.

10. Children's Privacy

Dronna is intended for users aged 13 years and above. Users under 18 should use the platform with parent or guardian consent and supervision.

11. Third-Party Links

Dronna may link to third-party websites or services, including Razorpay. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we recommend reviewing those policies before using third-party services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of Dronna after changes become effective means you accept the updated policy.
