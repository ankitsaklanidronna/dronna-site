Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains refund eligibility, cancellation handling, duplicate-payment support, failed-payment support, and refund timelines for Dronna digital courses.

Effective Date: May 14, 2026 | Last Updated: May 14, 2026
Dronna sells digital educational access. Refunds are reviewed within the timelines below and approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method.

1. Scope

This policy applies to payments made for Dronna digital course access, paid practice sets, premium tools, and related online learning services purchased through dronna.in.

2. Refund Eligibility

You may request a refund within 7 days of purchase if:

  • you were charged more than once for the same transaction;
  • payment was successful but paid access was not activated due to a technical issue;
  • you reported a major platform-access issue within the refund window and we could not resolve it within a reasonable time;
  • the purchased digital course was materially unavailable due to a Dronna-side issue; or
  • you have not consumed a significant portion of the paid content.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

  • refund request made after 7 days of purchase, except where required by law or approved by Dronna in exceptional cases;
  • extensive usage or substantial consumption of paid content;
  • account sharing, content copying, misuse, fraud, or violation of Dronna Terms;
  • dissatisfaction based only on exam results, marks, rank, selection, or personal outcomes;
  • change of mind after using the paid service; or
  • technical issues caused by unsupported devices, unstable internet, incorrect login details, or user-side configuration where Dronna support remains available.

4. Cancellations

Dronna mainly provides one-time digital course access rather than physical goods. Once paid access is activated, cancellation of the purchase does not automatically create a refund. If recurring subscriptions are introduced in the future, users will be able to request cancellation of future renewals by contacting support before the next billing date.

5. Duplicate or Failed Payments

If money was deducted but access was not activated, or if you were charged twice for the same purchase, email us with your registered Dronna email address, payment date, amount, and Razorpay Payment ID or transaction reference. Genuine duplicate payments will be refunded to the original payment method after verification.

6. How to Request a Refund

Please email support@dronna.in with:

  • your registered Dronna email address;
  • Razorpay Payment ID, order ID, or bank/UPI transaction reference;
  • date of purchase and amount paid;
  • course or feature purchased; and
  • reason for the refund request, with screenshots if available.

7. Review and Processing Timelines

  • We aim to acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days.
  • We may ask for additional details to verify the transaction and account ownership.
  • Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after approval.
  • Actual bank, UPI, card, or wallet credit timelines may vary depending on Razorpay, banks, card networks, and payment providers.

8. Refund Method

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the transaction. We do not issue cash refunds or transfer refunds to unrelated bank accounts, UPI IDs, wallets, or third-party accounts.

9. Chargebacks and Disputes

For payment disputes, duplicate charges, or access issues, please contact us first so we can investigate quickly. If a chargeback or bank dispute is raised, course access may be paused while the dispute is under review.

For faster resolution, include the Razorpay Payment ID or order ID in your first email.
